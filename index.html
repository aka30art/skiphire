HOW TO RUN YOUR COMPANY

1 · Contracts
Open Contracts and accept an offer. Check its skip size, estimated costs and reward before committing.

2 · Matching skips
Every active job reserves one skip of the same size. Two Medium jobs therefore require two Medium skips. Your overall fleet capacity can impose an additional limit.

3 · Complete the job
Deliver the skip, collect the waste, then complete the job to return the skip to your depot.

4 · Process and sell
Open Processing to recover metal, wood and cardboard. Sell your stock through the Material Market.

5 · Expand the company
Fleet manages skips and job capacity. More contains the Upgrade Centre, Automation and Company HQ for finance, reputation, staff, facilities, events and achievements.
